Inner Glow Experience

A calm base for Ubud, wrapped in green.

Each boho-styled villa is designed for practical comfort: a queen size bed, living area, meal prep space with fridge, work desk, and private bathroom. Guests also have space to breathe, slow down, and enjoy the quiet of the rice fields.

Here, the best of Ubud is close by, while tranquillity is right outside your window.
